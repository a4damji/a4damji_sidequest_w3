## Description

This p5.js project demonstrates a simple multi-screen game. This game includes various buttons throughout and multiple endings. It also has a counter which changes depending on the player's ending. The game gives you a task to collect a tomato for the tomato guy. You are taken to a tomato vine when you start where 6 options lay out before you. Each tomato randomizes a fresh or rotten result. There you can choose to repick or give the tomato to the tomato guy. The type you give to the Tomato guy will either negatively or positively impact your likeability score.

---

## Setup and Interaction Instructions:

To start game either press enter or click the start button. On the screen with a tomato vine click any of the tomatoes to get a randomized result. You can choose to give the tomato you selected to the guy or repick. Repicking will take you back to the tomato vine. Choosing the give takes you to one of two endings, there you can click esc, b/B, or the restart button to return to the begginning. A likeability counter is on the start screen and goes up for every good ending and goes down for every bad ending. Reloading page will refresh the counter.
